The pipeline

From counts matrix to biological insight.

Step 1 — 3

Preprocessing

Filter low-quality cells and rarely-expressed genes, then log1p-normalize and z-score-scale to remove sequencing-depth bias.

Step 4 — 5

Dimensionality

PCA reduces noise; UMAP and t-SNE preserve cell-cell similarity structure in two dimensions for visualization.

Step 6

Cluster & mark

K-means identifies cell populations; per-cluster Welch t-tests with log-FC ranking surface candidate cell-type markers.
